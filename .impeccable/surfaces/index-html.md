# Surface: alperensaglam.github.io home (and the shared app-page shell)

Mode: Read (a catalogue and support index). Audience and job: an app user or App Store reviewer looking for one app's how-to, contact or privacy policy, usually on an iPhone; secondarily someone evaluating what Alperen has shipped. Constraint: every existing app URL keeps resolving; legal text is never reworded; TR/EN on every page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Five shipped products presented the way Panic, Lux and Cultured Code present theirs: each app a confident product band with its real icon, a real screenshot and its three ways in (App Store, how to use, privacy). Refuses the same-size card grid and the dark neon developer portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with soft gray (#F5F5F7) bands, ink #1D1D1F and secondary #6E6E73. Each app band takes a pale tint of its own icon colour (Lokma orange, Drawluma pink, PuzzleLens blue, Biverba navy, Watchntalk amber) and uses that colour for its links. Mona Sans variable throughout (display 700 to 800, tight tracking; body 400). Real icons in the iOS squircle with a soft offset shadow; the official App Store badge.

STORY: The visitor sees their app's icon in the first viewport, reaches its band in one tap, then How to use or Privacy in the second. A reviewer finds any policy in one click from the support index. An evaluator sees five shipped products and who made them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with "Alperen Sağlam" left; Apps, Support, Contact and a TR/EN switch right. Headline about 64px on desktop with one intro sentence. Below it an icon shelf of five 88px app icons with names, which is the quick navigation. The first product band starts above the fold at 1440×900.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen by the user from the safer re-roll; quality bar Panic, Lux, Cultured Code; seed key 3545d878. Signature interaction: an icon in the shelf scrolls to its band, whose icon lifts once. The language switch swaps all copy in place and remembers the choice.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
