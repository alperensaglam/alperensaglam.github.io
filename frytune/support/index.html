Restoring FryTune Pro

Open the Settings tab in FryTune and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

I'm not getting cooking reminders

FryTune reminds you to shake, flip and check with notifications, even when the app is closed. If they don't arrive, check Settings → Notifications → FryTune and make sure notifications and Time Sensitive Notifications are allowed. The Settings tab in FryTune shows whether reminders are on.

The timer isn't on my Lock Screen

The Live Activity needs Settings → FryTune → Live Activities to be on. It appears once you start a cook.

Are the times exact?

No cooking time is exact: fryers and food vary. FryTune gives a range and a first check, and learns from your ratings. For chicken, meat and fish, always confirm doneness with a food thermometer — the target temperature is shown in each plan.

Deleting your data

In FryTune, Settings → Erase history and favourites deletes your cooking history. Everything FryTune stores lives only on your iPhone; deleting the app removes it. See the privacy policy.
