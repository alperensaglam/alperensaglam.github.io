FryTune Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

FryTune is an air fryer cooking assistant. It is designed to work without collecting any personal data.

What FryTune stores

Your air fryers, cooking history, ratings, notes, favourites and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. FryTune has no account system and no servers, and does not send this information anywhere. If you use iCloud or device backups, this data is included in your device backup like other app data.

Camera and photos

Smart Scan uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo, or a photo you pick from your library. The image is analysed on your iPhone (Apple's Vision framework) to suggest a food and read packaging text. Photos are not stored by FryTune and never leave your device.

Notifications

FryTune schedules local notifications on your iPhone for cooking steps. No push service is used.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. FryTune receives only the information needed to unlock Pro (whether a valid purchase exists); it does not receive your name, email or payment details.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

FryTune contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Kitchen tab → "Erase history and favourites" deletes your cooking history and favourites. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

FryTune is a general cooking utility and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
